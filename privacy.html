Privacy Policy

Effective: November 1, 2026 · Operated by: Corny Collectibles & Consulting LLC, doing business as MOBENTUM (“we”, “us”), Massachusetts, USA · Contact: coachcorn.dcsc@gmail.com

This policy explains what the M☺︎BENTUM app (“the app”) collects, how we use it, who can see it and your choices. It covers only the app.

1. Summary

  • You can use the app without an account. Your information then stays on your device.
  • If you sign in with your email, we also store your workout data in our database so it follows you across devices.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or build advertising or marketing profiles.
  • The app asks how you feel, including pain areas. We use those answers only to build and adjust your workouts and show your progress.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time in the app (Progress → Account → Delete my account).
  • M☺︎BENTUM offers general fitness information. It is not medical advice.

2. Information we collect

You give us:

  • Email address, if you sign in. Used to send a one-time sign-in code and identify your account.
  • Check-in answers: sleep, energy and stress ratings; body areas with pain or discomfort; available workout time; equipment; experience level.
  • Workout records: exercises completed, reps or seconds, weights you enter, how you felt afterward, points, streaks and settings (such as pounds or kilograms).
  • Messages you send us by email, and our replies.

Pain and how-you-feel answers are health-related information. Please do not enter anything you would not want stored. You can use the app without a pain answer.

Collected automatically: servers receive technical data when you load the app or sign in, such as IP address, device and browser type and request time. Our hosting and service providers may log it. We do not build profiles from it. The app also keeps data on your device (local storage and a cached copy of the app so it loads quickly or offline), including a sign-in session token if you sign in. The app does not use cookies of its own.

What we do not collect: payment information, precise location, contacts, photos or files, government identifiers. The app has no advertising or analytics tools.

3. How we use information

  • Create your daily workout and adjust difficulty from your check-in and past results.
  • Track and show your progress, levels, weights and points.
  • Sign you in and keep your data in sync across devices.
  • Send sign-in codes and answer your messages.
  • Keep the app working, secure and free of abuse; fix problems; comply with the law and enforce our terms.

We do not use your health-related answers for advertising, or to make decisions about eligibility, employment, insurance or credit.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your information. We share it only as follows:

  • Service providers that help us run the app: Supabase (database and sign-in); GitHub Pages (hosting the app); Google Gmail (sending sign-in emails); jsDelivr and unpkg (delivering a software library) and Google Fonts (fonts). These providers can see your IP address when your browser loads their files.
  • YouTube, only if you choose to play an exercise demo video. YouTube may collect information under its own policy.
  • Legal and safety: if required by law, subpoena or legal process, or to protect the rights, safety or property of users, the public or us.
  • Business changes: if the app is sold, merged or transferred, your information may transfer to the new owner, who must honor this policy or give you notice.

Our authorized team can view stored records in the database to operate, support and secure the app. We limit that access to what is needed.

5. How long we keep information

  • Data on your device stays until you clear it, sign out, or delete the app.
  • Account data stays until you delete your account. We may delete accounts that have been inactive for 24 months.
  • Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to respond and keep records.
  • Provider backups and logs may keep copies for a limited time after deletion.

6. Security

Data between your device and our servers uses HTTPS, and our database restricts each signed-in account to its own record. No system is perfectly secure. Keep your email account secure, since it is how you sign in. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you and authorities as the law requires.

7. Health-related information

M☺︎BENTUM is a general fitness app. It is not a healthcare provider and does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition. Pain and wellness answers are used only to choose exercises that avoid the areas you report and to scale intensity. We do not sell them or share them for advertising, or share them outside the providers listed in section 4.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Use without an account. Data then stays on your device only.
  • Sign out at any time.
  • Delete your account and data: in the app, go to Progress → Account → Delete my account. This permanently removes your sign-in and your stored workout record from our database. You can also email coachcorn.dcsc@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. Data stored only on your device is removed by clearing your browser or app data, or by removing the app from your home screen.
  • Access or correct your data: your data is visible in the app, and you can ask for a copy at coachcorn.dcsc@gmail.com.
  • We send emails only for sign-in and account matters. We do not send marketing emails.

9. Additional rights depending on where you live

Depending on your location, you may have the right to know what we collect, access and receive a copy of your information, correct or delete it, withdraw consent, and not be treated unfairly for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising. To make a request, email coachcorn.dcsc@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within the time the law allows. If we decline, you can ask how to appeal.

10. Children

The app is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, email coachcorn.dcsc@gmail.com and we will delete it.

11. International users

The app is operated in the United States and intended for users in the United States. Our providers may store and process information in the United States and other countries.

12. “Do Not Track” and cookies

The app does not track you across other sites or apps and does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

13. Third-party links

The app may link to other sites, such as video pages. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

14. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new effective date, and notify you in the app or by email before material changes take effect where the law requires.
